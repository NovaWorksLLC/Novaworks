A daily assistant concept

CallMe, branded CallBrief in the current prototype, explores a personal daily assistant with a call-style briefing experience.

Current prototype

The prototype includes onboarding, agreement screens, permission setup, and a home screen. The briefing, call, and scheduling actions are still under development.

Planned direction

The concept is intended to bring selected daily information into a convenient briefing. Specific integrations and permission requirements will be finalized as development progresses.
