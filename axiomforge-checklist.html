Review by project and drawing

Select a project and drawing, then load the applicable checklist. Drawing classification supports general arrangement, detail, and revision review definitions.

Discipline and role-specific items

Organize checks for drafting, mechanical engineering, process engineering, marketing engineering, and project management. Control which items apply to each role.

Administer company checklists

An administrator can add, edit, organize, and activate checklist definitions and items through the application. A shared company checklist source lets other installations use centrally maintained definitions.

Preserve review records

Save checklist completion and sign-off records to support a consistent review process. The standalone Windows application is in development and carries the AxiomForge branding.
